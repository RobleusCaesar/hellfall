What the audit found, in order of consequence:

- **The demons are 1.70 m tall, not 0.017 m.** The node parser had applied the armature's 0.01 scale to skinned bind buffers that glTF says to ignore; Blender's skinned rest-pose measurement is authoritative. Scale factors to target are **1.29x and 1.76x** (to 220 / 300 cm), not 130x / 176x. Both rigs are intact in Blender: one armature, 24 bones rooted at `Hips`, one deformed mesh, named actions with real frame ranges (ember 5 clips, crimson 10; the Gate 5 mapping is in `BUILD.md` 5.1). Whether the skinning survives the FBX round-trip stays the Gate 4 question.
- **The guard is a seated slump, not a prone body**: back to a wall, legs straight out 161 cm, 82 cm crown. Along a 280 corridor he leaves 202 cm (legs across: ~120), under the 216 cm prop minimum. Section 5 A2 records what was done about it (the spec placement is honoured under a body-only waiver).
- **Four models are not what their names suggest**: the toilet is a **wall-hung bowl** with exposed plumbing (pivot wall, box bottom 2 cm off the floor, rim at ~42); the reception desk is a **157 x 157 diorama** at desk height (75) with the office chair, monitor, cup and a fallen item baked into the one mesh; the kitchen set has **two** chairs, not four; the couch set is one **L-shaped** mesh (3-seat sofa, perpendicular loveseat, coffee table in front of the sofa), 280 x 254 x 80.
- **Every other static file is normalised to ~1.9 m on its longest axis** with its bounding-box centre at the origin; the Gate 4 pipeline moves every pivot (floor contact for 13 slots, wall contact for the door, elevator and toilet, muzzle for the shotgun).
- **Budgets:** every count fits its spec class except the couch set at **8,104 / 8,000 (1.3 % over, inside the 20 % tolerance)**; `broken_door` lands exactly on 4,000. No decimation beyond the Godot-era pass is needed at Gate 4. Triangle counts agree with the parser within 1 % for 16 of 17 files, and every difference equals the duplicate faces Blender's mesh validation drops (661 in `intern_sitting`: the flipped duplicates that fake two-sided hair cards - a two-sided hair material at Gate 4).
- **Rejected:** `crimson_hellfiend_texture_0_1.png` - a byte-identical duplicate of `crimson_hellfiend_texture_0.png` (same MD5; the GLB embeds both copies). **Removed** from `SourceAssets/` with `git rm` (LFS-tracked) for the gate-2 commit: the tree now holds **125 files** and contains no name matching ` - Copy`, `Meshy_AI_` or `deamon` (REQ-G2-001 acceptance 2). The spec's other expected rejections (` - Copy` duplicates, `deamon`, `Meshy_AI_*`, `shotgun2`, `ceo_dead2`, the third demon) were resolved by the earlier Godot pass that produced this working set; the raw 2.8 GB Meshy bundle is not on this machine, so those rows cannot be re-inspected and the Godot-era selection is inherited (`BUILD.md` section 5 "Provenance").
- **Importer artifact:** Blender adds an 80-triangle `Icosphere` bone widget per armature that is not in the GLB; the audit excludes it and the Gate 4 export must skip it.
